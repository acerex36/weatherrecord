Trip Source Risk Ranking

Pick a state, installation type, month and hour. The dashboard ranks the trip sources that have tripped most often under similar conditions, with their usual trip points and recorded causes.

Score = past trips + trips in the same month (±1) + trips at the same time of day (±3h). This is a ranking from history, not a validated forecast: in a back-test, the top 10 sources caught about 27% of later Kedah trips and about 15% of later Perlis trips.

Probability that a trip point that has already faulted faults again within N days. Ranked, not a calibrated certainty: PR-AUC 0.146 against a 0.074 base rate, so treat the bands as inspection priority. Consequence is a proxy from the trip point's role in the network (PMU / substation switchboard = Major, PPU / PDB = Moderate, service level = Minor), because the records carry no outage duration or customer count. Report it as a proxy, not a measurement.
